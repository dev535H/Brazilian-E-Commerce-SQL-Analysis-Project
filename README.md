# 🛒 E-Commerce SQL Analysis Project
## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset (Olist) using SQL.
It explores sales, customers, delivery performance, seller performance, and product ratings to extract business insights and key performance metrics.

## Dataset

- Customers, Orders, Order Items, Products, Sellers, Payments, Reviews

- Source: Olist E-Commerce Dataset

## Key Questions Answered

- Sales & Revenue: total revenue, top products, top categories, monthly sales trends

- Customer Analysis: top spenders, repeat buyers, average spending, top cities/states

- Delivery & Logistics: average delivery time, fastest/slowest states, late delivery percentage

- Seller Performance: top sellers by orders & revenue, sellers with high sales but low ratings

- Customer Satisfaction: overall average rating, best & worst-rated categories, percentage of 5-star reviews

- Advanced Insights: effect of delivery delays on review scores

## Tools & Skills

- PostgreSQL (joins, aggregations, subqueries)

- SQL Analytics

- Data-driven business insights

## Insights

- Sellers with high revenue but low ratings indicate service quality risk

- Delivery delays correlate with lower customer satisfaction

- Certain product categories dominate revenue

- Top customers contribute a large share of total sales
